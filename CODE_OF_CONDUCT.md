# Community guidelines

Railmora welcomes model railway builders of every experience level.

- Be constructive, specific and respectful.
- Critique ideas and software behaviour, not people.
- Keep discussions relevant to Railmora and model railway planning.
- Do not publish personal information, credentials, licence keys or private
  correspondence.
- Do not use the community for advertising, harassment or copyright
  infringement.

Content that violates these guidelines may be edited, hidden or removed.
Questions about moderation can be sent to
[info@railmora.eu](mailto:info@railmora.eu).
